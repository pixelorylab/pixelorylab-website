FAQ

Common
questions.

Quick answers about services, pricing, and how we work — so you can decide whether to book a call.

We specialize in motion graphics and video editing for SaaS brands, launch videos, product explainers, and ad creatives built to perform across your marketing channels.
Mostly SaaS founders and marketing teams, along with branding studios and creators who need consistent, high-quality video content on a recurring basis. We built this service specifically for pre Series A SaaS companies that need premium looking content without enterprise budgets or long agency timelines.
After your 60% deposit, we send a structured intake form where you share your brief, brand assets, UI references (Figma links or screenshots), and any inspiration videos. Script is optional at this stage. We then build a full Figma storyboard and refine the script with you. Before production begins, we hop on a short finalization call to confirm everything looks right. We then edit the full video, you get one revision round via Frame.io, and we deliver the final file.
Your brief, brand assets (logo, fonts, colors, brand guidelines), any UI references such as Figma links or screenshots of your product, and inspiration videos that match the style you're after. A script is optional, if you don't have one, we'll write it together.
Each video is delivered within 10 business days of receiving your completed assets, with rush delivery in 5 business days available for an additional flat fee. Retainer work runs on an ongoing cadence we agree on upfront.
One revision round is included on every project. You review the draft via Frame.io and leave timestamped comments, we apply your changes and deliver the final file. Additional revision rounds are available for a flat fee.
Project based pricing runs from $800 to $5,500 per video depending on complexity and the editor assigned. Retainer plans start at $6,000 per month for four videos a month with priority turnaround. Get in touch for a quote.
